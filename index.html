ABOUT THIS STUDY

Purpose of the Study

What is FCR?

FCR is fear, worry, or concern about the possibility that cancer will come back or progress. It affects approximately 50% of adolescent and young adult (AYA) cancer survivors aged 15–39, and is the most frequently reported unmet supportive care need.

Impact

FCR is associated with psychological distress, reduced quality of life, and avoidance of follow-up care.

Our Goal

This study pilot-tests an AI chatbot, powered by a large language model (a type of AI system that generates text), designed to provide 24-hour FCR support to AYA cancer survivors in Hong Kong.

We want to find out

  • Is the chatbot feasible, acceptable, and usable?
  • What are its potential effects on FCR, mood, and resilience?
Pilot test: This study is an early test of whether an FCR-focused AI chatbot is feasible, acceptable, usable, and helpful for AYA cancer survivors in Hong Kong.

CHATBOT FOCUS

When to Use the Chatbot

FAITH-AYA is designed for psychological and emotional support when you experience fear, worry, or concern about cancer recurrence (FCR).

Best time to use it

Start a conversation when FCR comes up, such as when you feel worried that cancer may come back or progress. The chatbot can help you reflect, feel heard, and practise strategies that may reduce FCR.

What it is not for

This is not a treatment consultation or a service for finding medical resources. Although the chatbot may include general information, do not use it for diagnosis, treatment decisions, medication advice, or urgent medical concerns.

The focus of this pilot test is whether using this FCR-focused chatbot can help reduce FCR. It is not a comparison with a general chatbot.
4-WEEK PROGRAMME

What You'll Explore

Week 1

Introduction and Needs Assessment

  • FCR theories
  • Emotional validation
Week 2

Goal Setting and Detachment

  • Values clarification and goal setting
  • Benefits of successful detachment from cancer survivors
  • Detachment methods: mindfulness, exercise, yoga, and relaxation
Week 3

Learning to Live Well and Manage Worry

  • Videos from cancer survivors
  • Worry-management techniques
  • Compliance with follow-up care and self-examination
Week 4

Relapse Plan and Empowerment

  • Reflection on changes in FCR
  • Suggested strategies for managing FCR through relapse prevention
  • Empower participants to manage FCR through emotional validation
