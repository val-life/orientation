YOUR AI COMPANION

Meet Your AI Chatbot

👋 Hi! I'm here to support you throughout the next four weeks.
I'm worried about my next follow-up appointment.
Many cancer survivors feel this way. Would you like to try a short breathing exercise together?

The chatbot can support you by...

💙 Listening to your concerns
🌱 Teaching coping strategies
🧘 Guiding relaxation exercises
🎯 Encouraging healthy goals
📚 Sharing reliable information
⚠ This chatbot is designed to provide emotional support. It is **not** a replacement for medical advice.
